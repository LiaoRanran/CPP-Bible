## 1. 必须降格的残余（3 处，附可直接粘贴的替换文本）

> 说明：这三句都不是"虚假"，而是**措辞比证据宽**。评审读摘要只读这几句，
> 所以它们的影响面远大于正文里的同义句。

### R1 · P2 摘要 L86：`a large class … at all`

**现文**：

> We show that **a large class** of evaluation failures requires **no optimisation pressure at all**:
> the number changes because the *caliber* of measurement changed, while the capability being measured
> does not.

**问题**：`a large class` 是**范围主张**，但全文的理论结果是一个**八公理模型内的构造性刻画**，
并没有对"evaluation failures 的全体"做过计数或覆盖证明；`at all` 又加强了排他性。

**建议替换**：

> We exhibit **a class of** evaluation failures that requires **no optimisation pressure**:
> the number changes because the *caliber* of measurement changed, while the capability being measured
> does not.

（把 `show that a large class … at all` 改成 `exhibit a class of …`——**"展示一类"是可证的，"很大一类"不是**。）

### R2 · P2 摘要 L95–96：`close it` + `settle a completeness criterion`

**现文**：

> The seven-axiom core is incomplete… and we **close it** with an eighth axiom (label-axis closure),
> whose necessity we prove by exhaustive enumeration over all 15 label mappings of the four-sample
> witness domain, and **settle a completeness criterion** for the extended signature.

**问题**：`close it` 与 `settle` 读起来像"一般性完结"，但紧随其后的限定语自己说明了范围只是
**four-sample witness domain** 的 **15 个标签映射**。而且（与 714 批登记一致）A8 的闭包**没有自动验证**：
它是"有限域穷举 + 人写规范"，不是机器检查的完备性证明。

**建议替换**：

> …and we **extend** it with an eighth axiom (label-axis closure), whose necessity we establish
> **by exhaustive enumeration over the 15 label mappings of the four-sample witness domain**
> (a finite model check, not a general proof), and we **propose** a completeness criterion for the
> extended signature **and register the check that would falsify it**.

（三处改动：`close`→`extend`、`settle`→`propose`、并显式标注 `a finite model check, not a general proof`。
最后半句把"没做自动验证"变成**已登记的待办**，而不是隐含的缺口。）

### R3 · P2 摘要 L101：`possible exactly when`

**现文**：

> **Correctability**: post-hoc repair is **possible exactly when** the drift acts on the
> post-measurement layer; acquisition-layer drift requires re-measurement, with residual error up to 30.0%.

**问题**：`exactly when` 是**双向充要**的强表述。若它确实是模型内的定理，请显式写出模型；
否则被反问一句"你证明了逆命题吗"就会很难答。

**建议替换**：

> **Correctability**: **within the model**, post-hoc repair is possible **iff** the drift acts on the
> post-measurement layer (both directions proved in \S\ref{sec:correct}); acquisition-layer drift requires
> re-measurement, with residual error up to 30.0%.

（要么补上"双向已证 + 章节号"，要么降为 `possible when`。**二选一，但不要留着含糊的 `exactly when`**。）
