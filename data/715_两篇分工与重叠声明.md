> 论文 2 写的 "No experimental results are duplicated"，**紧接着列的四项恰恰就是重复项**

> No experimental results are duplicated: the transition counts, the T1/T5/T6 diagnostics, the causal

## 3. 修正后的重叠声明（任务卡第 1 条：**如实写出哪些结果是共享的**）

论文 2 的 `Overlap with the companion submission` 段**整段替换**为：

> Both papers read the same frozen matrices (the $1147\times8$ verdict matrix, the $566$-sample paired
> split), because neither runs new measurement. They differ in what they extract: the companion reports
> *findings* about one apparatus (what the audit found, and the protocol that found it); this paper
> reports *boundaries* on auditing in general (what any such audit can and cannot establish).
> **Four numbers therefore appear in both, in different roles:** the $566$-frame transition counts
> (here, tests of T1/T3/T4 and Theorem 3; there, the protocol's transition matrix); the
> sample-complexity table (here, contribution C3; there, the protocol consequence that Step 3 becomes a
> dual design); the transfer score (here, validated against criterion I1–I5; there, a one-paragraph
> answer to whether the rules travel); and the selection-gain decomposition (here, an illustration of
> Corollary 2.1's non-identifiability---it is read *negatively*, as a limit; there, Finding F1, read
> *positively*, as a finding). The axiomatisation, the identifiability and correctability theorems, the
> sample-complexity derivation and the transfer criterion appear *only* here.

**四条共享项及其"角色差"（这就是修正的核心）**：

| 共享的数 | 论文 1 里的角色 | 论文 2 里的角色 |
|---|---|---|
| 566 帧转移计数 | 协议的转移矩阵（工具） | T1/T3/T4 与定理 3 的**检验** |
| 样本复杂度表 | 协议推论（Step 3 变双设计） | 贡献 **C3**（定理级） |
| 迁移分 55.84→78.26 | 一段话回答"规则会不会迁移" | I1–I5 判据**验证**的对象 |
| 选择增益分解 | Finding **F1**（正面读数） | Cor. 2.1 不可识别性的**负面**例证（当作"极限"） |

**把"either new or reported in a different role"这种含糊表述，换成逐条列出"在两边各是什么角色"**。
