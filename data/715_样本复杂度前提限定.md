## 2. 已写进论文的**全部前提假设**（逐条）

论文 2 贡献 C3 现在的完整表述（修改后）：

> **C3 (sample complexity of an audit, conditional on measured $\psi$).** Paired designs have identically
> zero power against Type III and Type IV drift ($\psi=0$, hence $n=\infty$)---a property of the
> **design**, not of the budget. For the two detectable types, $n=m/\psi$ at $\alpha=0.05$ and power
> $0.8$, which on our apparatus reads $n=849$ (Type I) and $n=17$ (Type II); the $\psi$-independent
> information-theoretic floors are the **KL bounds ($329$ and $7$)**. The reading rests on measured
> $\psi$, so the cost is governed by the discordance rate, not the effect size; $849$ and $17$ are
> point estimates that move with the asset pool and the environment pair---**a conditional design
> analysis, not a sample-size requirement for audit studies in general.**

## 3. 三个数各自的身份（这是本节的核心）

| 数 | 身份 | 会不会随池/环境变 |
|---|---|---|
| **KL 地板 $329$（Type I）/ $7$（Type II）** | **$\psi$-无关的信息论下界** | 不随 $\psi$ 变；但仍**在本模型的假设内** |
| **$n=849$（Type I）** | $n=m/\psi$ 的**点估计**，$\psi$ 取实测值 | ✅ **会变** |
| **$n=17$（Type II）** | 同上 | ✅ **会变** |

**关键更正**：修改前 $849/17$ 被放在 "information-theoretic bound" 标题下，读者会当成下界；
修改后明确"**$\psi$-无关的地板是 KL 界，$849/17$ 是读自实测 $\psi$ 的点估计**"。

论文 2 §5 结论句同步改为：

> The structural rule---$n=m/\psi$, so audit budgets should be planned by **how many samples carry the
> drift**, not by how large the drift looks---is domain-independent and is the content of C3. The two
> numbers that illustrate it ($849$ versus $17$) are read off **this** apparatus: replace the asset
> pool or the environment pair and both numbers change, and the ratio $50\times$ is a ratio of measured
> $\psi$, not an intrinsic difficulty ordering of the two types.

**$50\times$ 这个比值的身份**也被写明：它是**实测 $\psi$ 之比**，不是"Type I 天生比 Type II 难 50 倍"。

论文 1 附录 `app:samplecomplexity` 的对应句改为（新增加粗部分）：

> Two consequences follow, both **read off this apparatus** (566-sample frame, one environment pair,
> fully one-directional discordance $\pi{=}1$). (i) … **These two numbers are computed from measured
> $\psi$ and move with the asset pool and the environment pair; they are not universal constants.**
> The derivation, the KL floors ($329$ and $7$), and the strategy simulations are in the companion
> paper…
