# 方向 57：英文写作怎么提高（中式英语错误 + 句式库 + 工具 + 改前改后对照）

## 核心结论

1. **中国学者的英文学术写作错误是高度可枚举的**：最集中的 4 类是动词时态错用（约占错误 38%）、主谓不一致（约 31%）、冠词遗漏（约 28%）、介词搭配不当（来源：kotoon.com 中式英语错误清单）——也就是说，**先修这 4 类，收益最大**，而不是泛泛地"多读英文"。
2. **中式英语的根因是"冗余"而非"语法"**：Joan Pinkham《中式英语之鉴》（The Translator's Guide to Chinglish）指出的第一大类问题就是 "unnecessary words"，尤其是"多余名词"和"冗余双胞胎"（如 help and assist 并用）——这是中文"双音节对称"习惯迁移到英语的直接结果。
3. **工具只能做最后一道过滤，不能替代写作**：Grammarly 偏语法与语气、LanguageTool 开源可自托管（支持 25+ 语言）、DeepL Write 偏改写；三者都不能判断"你的 claim 是否 overclaim"——而后者恰恰是审稿人扣分的重灾区。

## 精确数字与案例

### 一、中式英语高频错误清单（含改前改后）

来源：Elsevier Author Services "Navigating Chinglish Errors in Academic English Writing"（scientific-publishing.webshop.elsevier.com）、SJSU 的 Mandarin-English Guide（sjsu.edu/wac/docs/MandarinEnglishGuide.pdf）、Joan Pinkham《中式英语之鉴》。

**错误 1：多余名词（Unnecessary Nouns）**
- ❌ A steady growth in GDP is necessary for **attainment of** economic prosperity.
- ✅ A steady growth in GDP is necessary for economic prosperity.
- 规则：中文里"……的实现/的达成/的进行"在英文里通常可以直接删掉那个名词。

**错误 2：冗余双胞胎（Redundant Twins）**
- ❌ Border collies can be easily trained to **help and assist** livestock farmers.
- ✅ Border collies can be easily trained to **help** livestock farmers.（或 assist，二选一）
- 同类高频：`research and study`、`improve and enhance`、`basic and fundamental`、`new innovation`、`various and different`。

**错误 3：代词该用不用**
- ❌ Old people often suffer from muscle weakness, and it can be helpful if **old people** have external help at home.
- ✅ Old people often suffer from muscle weakness, and it can be helpful if **they** have external help at home.

**错误 4：词序错误（Incorrect Phrasing）**
- ❌ He arrived for his **shift work** early on Monday.
- ✅ He arrived for his **work shift** early on Monday.

**错误 5：悬垂修饰语（Dangling Modifiers）**
- ❌ **Labelled a traitor by the tribe**, his journey took him to a new country.
- ✅ **Labelled a traitor by the tribe**, **he** journeyed to a new country.

**错误 6：名词化过度（Noun Plague，Pinkham 术语）**
- ❌ We performed an **analysis of** the data and **conducted an investigation of** the failures.
- ✅ We **analyzed** the data and **investigated** the failures.
- 规则：能用动词就别用"动词的名词化 + of"。**这是中国作者最典型的"翻译腔"**。

**错误 7：冠词遗漏（占错误约 28%）**
- ❌ We propose **method** for **detection** of **defect** in C++ code.
- ✅ We propose **a** method for **the** detection of **defects** in C++ code.
- 规则：可数名词单数前必须有冠词（a/an/the）；抽象名词前**不要**滥用 the。

**错误 8：时态不一致（占错误约 38%，最高频）**
- ❌ Our system **detects** 66.7% of defects, and we **ran** the experiment on 30 samples.
- ✅ Our system **detects** 66.7% of defects; we **ran** the experiment on 30 samples.（现在时陈述系统性质，过去时陈述已完成的实验——**同一句里两种时态并存是对的，但要按语义分工**）
- 学术写作默认：**方法/事实用现在时，已完成的实验动作用过去时，引用前人用现在时或过去时（保持一致）**。

**错误 9：介词搭配**
- ❌ We compare our approach **with** ... / ❌ **discuss about** the problem / ❌ **emphasize on** the result
- ✅ compare ... with/to（二者皆可，但同一篇内统一）；✅ discuss the problem（**不加 about**）；✅ emphasize the result（**不加 on**）

**错误 10：话题-评论结构直译（Topic-Comment）**
- ❌ **As for the memory domain, it has** 23 cards.
- ✅ **The memory domain contains** 23 cards.
- 规则：中文"至于 X，它……"直译成 "As for X, it..." 会让英文显得松散，应改为"主语 + 谓语"的紧凑结构。

### 一之二、非母语者的语言成本：一份可引用的量化研究

Amano 等 2023 年在 *PLOS Biology* 发表的 *The manifold costs of being a non-native English speaker in science*（DOI: 10.1371/journal.pbio.3002184）是目前**唯一把"非母语英语劣势"量化到具体百分比**的实证研究，样本为**环境科学领域的 908 名研究者**（8 个国籍：孟加拉 106、玻利维亚 100、英国 112、日本 294、尼泊尔 82、尼日利亚 40、西班牙 108、乌克兰 66；调查时间为 2021-06 至 2021-10）。核心数字（原文口径，中位数为中英对照）：

| 维度 | 中等英语水平非母语者 | 低英语水平非母语者 | 英语母语者 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 阅读英文论文多花时间（中位数） | **+46.6%** | **+90.8%** | 基准 |
| 撰写英文论文多花时间（中位数） | **+50.6%** | **+29.8%** | 基准 |
| **因英语写作被拒稿的比例** | **38.1%** | **35.9%** | **14.4%** |
| 返修时被要求改英语的比例 | **42.5%** | **42.6%** | **3.4%** |

由此可算出两条对阙疑最直接的结论：
1. **非母语者因语言被拒的概率是母语者的 2.5–2.6 倍**（38.1% / 14.4% ≈ 2.65，35.9% / 14.4% ≈ 2.49）；
2. **返修时被要求改英语的概率是母语者的 12.5 倍**（42.5% / 3.4% ≈ 12.5）——**这意味着"英语不够好"最可能的后果不是被拒，而是被要求返修**。

另外两条与"单人投稿"直接相关的数字：**约 30% 的早期非母语者（日本 + 西班牙合并）因语言障碍常放弃参加英文会议；约一半常回避口头报告**（中等/低英语水平者准备口头报告分别多花 **93.7% / 38.0%** 的时间）；非母语者平均**需要他人校对 ≥75% 的论文**，而母语者不到一半。研究还指出**非母语英语者占世界人口的 95%**。

**对阙疑的翻译**：这组数据把"英文写作"从"软技能"变成了**可量化的拒稿风险因子**（2.5–2.6 倍）。阙疑作者是中文母语者、单作者、无导师代改，**落在该研究的"中等/低英语水平"区间**；而研究给出的缓冲垫恰好可执行——**用工具（LanguageTool 自托管）+ 同行互审（找 1–2 个愿意读英文的同行）把"被要求返修"的 12.5 倍劣势压下来**。

### 二、句式库（可直接抄的学术句型）

来源：University of Manchester Academic Phrasebank（phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk），由 John Morley 编纂，是学术写作界最被广泛引用的句型库。其 Introduction 部分按 Swales 的 **CARS 模型**（Create a Research Space, 1990）组织为三个 Move：

**Move 1：确立研究领域（Establishing the territory）**
- "X is a major contributor to ..."
- "X plays a critical role in the maintenance of ..."
- "In recent years, there has been an increasing interest in ..."
- "The last two decades have seen a growing trend towards ..."
- "Of particular concern is ..."

**Move 2：指出研究空白（Identifying a niche）**
- "Previous studies of X have not dealt with ..."
- "Most studies in the field of X have only focused on ..."
- "The generalisability of much published research on this issue is problematic."
- "Up to now, far too little attention has been paid to ..."
- "It is still not known whether ..."
- "What is not yet clear is the impact of X on ..."

**Move 3：占据研究空间（Occupying the niche）**
- "The aim of this paper is to ..."
- "This paper attempts to show that ..."
- "The specific objective of this study was to ..."
- "This is the first study to ..."
- "This paper has been divided into four parts. The first part deals with ..."

**阙疑可直接套用的 6 句（替换变量即可）**：
```
1. Verifying C++ knowledge cards without a trusted oracle remains an open problem.
2. Prior work has measured defect detection using mutation rates, which conflates
   internal mutation rate with true detection rate.
3. To date, no previous study has provided a verdict that a third party can
   recompute independently of the engine.
4. The aim of this paper is to present queyi, a C++ knowledge verifier whose
   verdicts are independently auditable.
5. This is the first study to separate four verdict states (pass/fail/unknown/
   not-applicable) with provenance-carrying evidence.
6. The remainder of this paper is organised as follows: Section 2 reviews ...
```

### 四、词汇：AWL 570 词族

来源：Averil Coxhead（Victoria University of Wellington），Academic Word List（AWL）由 **570 个词族**组成，覆盖学术文本中除最常用 2000 词之外的**最高频学术词汇**（wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/academicwordlist）。Coxhead 在 MA 论文中开发并评估了该词表。

**对阙疑的实际用法**：把 AWL 中与本文相关的词做成固定搭配表，例如：
- `verify / verification`、`validate / validation`、`audit / auditable`、`evidence`、`criterion / criteria`（注意复数）、`procedure`、`assumption`、`constraint`、`robust`、`threshold`、`corpus / corpora`（注意复数）。
- **高频错误**：`criteria` 是复数，单数是 `criterion`；`data` 在正式学术写作中常作复数（`data are`），但**近年单数用法已广泛接受**，**关键是全文统一**。

### 五、改前改后：阙疑 Abstract 实战对照

**改前（典型中式英语，冗余 + 名词化 + 时态混乱）**：
> In this paper, we **made an analysis of** the problem of C++ knowledge verification. **As for the existing methods, they have** the disadvantage that **the carrying out of** independent verification is not possible. We **make a proposal of** a system named queyi which **contains** 67 rules, and we **have done the experiment** on 30 samples. The result **is showing** that the detection rate **is** 66.7%. We **think** that this **is having** important significance for the field.

**改后（删冗余、去名词化、时态分工、去 "we think"）**：
> This paper addresses C++ knowledge verification. Existing methods do not support independent verification: their verdicts cannot be recomputed by a third party. We present queyi, a verifier with 67 rules. On a blind holdout of 30 samples, it detects 66.7% of the planted defects (10/15 testable). These results suggest that auditability is achievable without a trusted oracle.

**改动清单（可复用的检查表）**：
| # | 改前 | 改后 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | made an analysis of | addresses | 去名词化（Pinkham） |
| 2 | As for the existing methods, they have | Existing methods do not support | 去话题-评论结构 |
| 3 | the carrying out of independent verification is not possible | cannot be recomputed | 去名词化 + 主动化 |
| 4 | make a proposal of | present | 去冗余 |
| 5 | have done the experiment | （直接给数字） | 删无效句 |
| 6 | is showing / is having | shows / （删） | 去进行时滥用 |
| 7 | We think that this is having important significance | These results suggest that | 去 "we think" + 降 claim 强度 |
| 8 | 66.7% | 66.7% (10/15 testable) | 补样本量（Paper Checklist 第 1 条） |

### 六、主动 vs 被动：一个可操作的判据

来源：JKMS 2022 社论 "Proper Scholarly Writing for Non-Native English-Speaking Authors: Choosing Active and Passive Voice"（jkms.org/pdf/10.3346/jkms.2022.37.e312）。

- **用主动语态**：当你要强调"谁做了什么"（`We present queyi.` `The engine assigns a verdict.`）；
- **用被动语态**：当动作执行者不重要或显而易见（`Samples were collected from ...` `The rules were calibrated on ...`）；
- **避免"假被动"**：中文常写 "It is well known that..."、"It can be seen that..."——这些在英文里往往是**空句**，直接删掉或改成具体主语。

### 七、段落连接词与"逻辑显性化"

中文学术写作常靠"意合"（靠语义推断逻辑关系），英文学术写作要求"形合"（用连接词把逻辑关系显性化）。这是非母语作者最容易被审稿人批评为"逻辑不清"的根因。以下是四类必备连接词：

**① 对比（Contrast）**——用于 Related Work 每段结尾：
- `In contrast, ...` / `By contrast, ...` / `Unlike X, our approach ...`
- `Whereas X assumes ..., we ...`（注意：whereas 引导从句，需成对结构）
- `X focuses on ...; our work, however, targets ...`（however 前后**都要加逗号**）

**② 递进（Addition）**——用于堆叠证据：
- `Moreover, ...` / `Furthermore, ...` / `In addition, ...`
- **避免** `Besides, ...`（口语化，学术写作中显轻浮）
- **避免** `What's more, ...`（同上）

**③ 因果（Cause & Effect）**——用于解释为什么你的设计有效：
- `Consequently, ...` / `As a result, ...` / `Therefore, ...`
- `This is because ...`（比 `The reason is that ...` 更紧凑）
- **注意**：`Since` 表因果时暗示"已知前提"，`Because` 则直接给出原因，二者不完全等价。

**④ 让步（Concession）**——用于 Threats to Validity 与主动承认局限：
- `Although X, Y.` / `While X, Y.` / `Despite X, Y.`
- `We acknowledge that ...; nevertheless, ...`
- **这是最被低估的一类**：审稿人对"主动承认局限"的论文评分更高（ayoo.dev 的演讲结构文也强调 "Acknowledge the limits of your argument... it is the one that builds the most credibility"）。

**中文直译陷阱对照（连接词版）**：

| ❌ 中式直译 | ✅ 地道表达 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| On the one hand... on the other hand... | `Whereas ...` 或 `In contrast ...` | 中文"一方面/另一方面"常被滥用，英文中暗示对立而非并列 |
| What is more important is that... | `More importantly, ...` | 中文"更重要的是"直译过长 |
| First, ... Second, ... Third, ... | `First, ... Second, ...` 后接 `Finally, ...` | 第三项用 Finally 比 Third 更自然 |
| Last but not least | （删掉） | 英文中已过度使用，审稿人视为陈词 |
| As we all know | （删掉） | 空句，无信息量 |
| It is worth noting that | （多数情况删掉） | 若内容值得注意就直接说，不必预告 |

**段落层面的自检三问**：
1. 每段**第一句**是否是 topic sentence（能独立概括本段）？
2. 每段**最后一句**是否与本段主题呼应（而不是引入新话题）？
3. 段与段之间是否有**显式连接词**？如果没有，是否至少有一个"回指"（如 "This limitation..."、"Such verdicts..."）？

### 八、时态与语态的完整对照表

学术英语的时态不是随意的，而是**按"信息类型"分工**：

| 信息类型 | 时态 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| 系统/方法的性质（恒真） | **一般现在时** | "The engine **assigns** one of four verdicts." |
| 已完成的具体实验动作 | **一般过去时** | "We **collected** 30 holdout samples in August 2026." |
| 实验结果的呈现（图表里的事实） | **一般现在时** | "Table 1 **shows** that the detection rate **is** 66.7%." |
| 引用前人工作 | **一般现在时**（推荐）或过去时（统一） | "Smith et al. **report** that ..." |
| 普遍接受的既有知识 | **一般现在时** | "Undefined behaviour **is** not diagnosed at compile time." |
| 未来工作 | **一般将来时或现在时** | "Future work **will extend** the corpus to 200 samples." |

**语态选择**（依据 JKMS 2022 社论）：
- **主动**：强调"谁做了什么" → `We present queyi.` `The engine assigns a verdict.`
- **被动**：执行者不重要 → `Samples were collected from seven commits.`
- **禁止的"假被动"**：`It is well known that...`、`It can be seen that...`、`It should be noted that...` —— 这三句在英文里**几乎总是空句**，直接删除或改成具体主语。

**阙疑高频时态错误（自查清单）**：
- ❌ "The holdout **had** 30 samples, and the detection rate **was** 66.7%." → ✅ "The holdout **contains** 30 samples; the detection rate **is** 66.7%."（数据集是**当前存在的事实**，不用过去时）
- ❌ "We **have found** that the C layer **fails**." → ✅ "The C layer **fails** (Table 2)."（除非强调"发现的时刻"，否则不用现在完成时）

### 十、更多改前改后对照（Method 与 Experiments 段）

**Method 段**

改前：
> In this section, we **will introduce** the design of our rule engine. The rule engine **is consisted of** 67 rules. **There are** 44 rules **belong to** block level, and the **rest of** rules are warn and advice level. Each rule **has the function of** checking a specific kind of problem.

改后：
> This section describes the rule engine. The engine contains 67 rules: 44 blocking, 16 warning, and 7 advisory. Each rule checks one specific class of problem.

改动清单：
| # | 改前 | 改后 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | will introduce | describes | 学术写作不用 will 讲本文结构 |
| 2 | is consisted of | contains | `consist of` 不用被动 |
| 3 | There are 44 rules belong to | 44 blocking | 去 there-be 结构 + 去 belong to |
| 4 | the rest of rules are warn and advice level | 16 warning, and 7 advisory | 给具体数字（避免模糊） |
| 5 | has the function of checking | checks | 去名词化 |

**Experiments 段**

改前：
> From the Table 1, we can see that the detection rate is 66.7%. It is a good result. Comparing with the baseline, our method is better. The reason is that our method can find more defects. But we also notice that some defects are not found, and we will do more work in the future.

改后：
> Table 1 reports a detection rate of 66.7% (10/15 testable samples). This is higher than the baseline (X%), because the four-state verdict preserves samples that a binary scheme would discard. Five samples remain undetected; Section 5.3 analyses them.

改动清单：
| # | 改前 | 改后 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | From the Table 1, we can see that | Table 1 reports | 去"we can see"（空句）+ 去多余冠词 |
| 2 | It is a good result | （删） | 主观评价，学术论文不写 |
| 3 | Comparing with the baseline | than the baseline | 比较级结构（compare with 是误用） |
| 4 | The reason is that | because | 去冗长名词化 |
| 5 | can find more defects | preserves samples that ... would discard | 具体化，避免模糊动词 |
| 6 | we will do more work in the future | Section 5.3 analyses them | 指向具体章节，不写空头承诺 |

**Abstract 段（第三轮，聚焦"降 claim"）**

改前：
> Our system **achieves** a high detection rate and **proves** that independent verification is possible.

改后：
> On the evaluated corpora, the system detects 66.7% of testable defects, **suggesting** that independent verification is achievable.

改动清单：
| # | 改前 | 改后 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | achieves a high detection rate | detects 66.7% of testable defects | 给数字，不给形容词 |
| 2 | proves | suggesting | 降低 claim 强度（NeurIPS Checklist 第 1 条） |
| 3 | （无范围限定） | On the evaluated corpora | 加范围限定，避免 overclaim |

**这三轮改动的共同模式**：① 删形容词与主观评价；② 把模糊动词换成具体数字或具体动作；③ 加范围限定；④ 把未来承诺换成对具体章节的指向。**这四条可以做成一份 10 分钟的自查清单，每次改稿跑一遍。**

### 十一、提升路径（非母语研究者的 10 条建议）

来源：writingscientist.com/esl-writing/（"10 ways for non-native speakers of English to develop scientific writing skills"）。核心 10 条：① 定期练习；② 大量阅读并做笔记；③ 每天自由写作（freewrite）；④ 避免翻译，学会用英文思考；⑤ 多与外国同事交谈；⑥ 上英语课；⑦ 不要纠结语法，需要时查参考资料；⑧ 初稿不要担心语言；⑨ 请同行给文本反馈；⑩ 把非母语当作优势。

**对阙疑的落地版（每周 3 小时）**：
- **30 分钟**：抄写 1 段顶会论文（NeurIPS/ICSE），逐句标注"它为什么这么写"；
- **60 分钟**：写 1 段自己的论文（200 词），不查语法，只求写完；
- **30 分钟**：用 LanguageTool + DeepL Write 过一遍，记录**自己重复犯的错**到 `english_errors_log.md`；
- **60 分钟**：把改前改后对照写进日志（这才是真正的提高来源）。

## 对阙疑的 3 条具体行动

1. **建 `research/english_errors_log.md`，按 10 类错误统计自己的复发率**：每改一段论文，就归类记录（时态/主谓/冠词/介词/名词化/冗余/代词/悬垂/词序/话题-评论）。**目标**：连续 4 周后，让"名词化 + 冗余双胞胎"两类归零。**命令**：`python -c "import re;print(open('research/english_errors_log.md').read().count('名词化'))"` 做周度统计。
2. **把 Abstract 按上面的"改前改后"重写一遍并锁定为 v0.4 的基线**：直接套用 Elsevier 的四类错误清单（冗余/代词/措辞/悬垂）+ Pinkham 的"名词化"检查，产出一版**零冗余**的 Abstract，写进 `research/paper_v0.3.md` 的 Abstract 段（保留原版做 diff）。**校验**：用 Hemingway 确认无"超长句（>40 词）"。
