# 方向 03：AI 文本检测的对抗方法

## 核心结论

1. **"对抗性改写"（Adversarial Paraphrasing）已经是学术主流攻击范式，且效果接近摧毁性。** Cheng、Sadasivan、Saberi、Saha、Feizi（马里兰大学，NeurIPS 2025，arXiv:2506.07001）提出一种**免训练**攻击：用一个现成 instruction-following LLM，在**检测器本身当奖励信号**的引导下改写 AI 文本，产生"专门优化来绕过检测"的对抗样本。结果：在 OpenAI-RoBERTa-Large 引导下，把 T@1%F 从基线**降低 64.49%（RADAR）**和**98.96%（Fast-DetectGPT）**，跨神经网络 / 水印 / 零样本检测器**平均降低 87.88%**。反讽的是，**朴素改写反而提高检测率**——同一实验里简单 paraphrase 让 RADAR 的 T@1%F **上升 8.57%**、Fast-DetectGPT **上升 15.03%**。这说明"改写"本身不是武器，**"以检测器为目标函数的定向优化"**才是。

2. **RL 化的改写攻击已把多检测器集成的平均 AUROC 从 0.79 压到 0.43，攻击成功率 97.6%。** StealthRL（Suraj Ranganath、Atharv Ramesh，arXiv:2602.08934，v1 2026-02-09 / v2 2026-03-19）用 **GRPO + LoRA 适配器微调 Qwen3-4B** 作为改写策略，在 MAGE 测试池（**15,310 条人类 / 14,656 条 AI**）上对 **RoBERTa、Fast-DetectGPT、Binoculars、MAGE 四个检测器**做集成攻击，得到 **平均 TPR@1%FPR = 0.024**、**平均 AUROC 0.79 → 0.43**、**攻击成功率 97.6%**，且**能迁移到训练时未见过的两个 held-out 检测器**——作者据此判断这是"共享的架构脆弱性"，而非检测器个体缺陷。

3. **但"能不能骗过检测器"与"能不能骗过 Pangram"是两件事：商业化 humanizer 在 Pangram 上几乎全军覆没。** Pangram 官方 2025-08-27 的 humanizer 基准（作者 Bradley Emi）审计了 **20 个 humanizer**，Pangram 对其**全部 >90% 检出**：Undetectable AI 90.3%、TwainGPT 92.7%、Just Done 93.5%、humanizeai.io 93.8%、StealthGPT 95.6%、DIPPER 97.6%，Ahrefs / Grammarly / Quillbot / Smodin / Ghost AI 等 **100.0%**。同页引用 Russell et al.（arXiv:2501.15654）：在 humanized 文本上 **Pangram 97% 准确，GPTZero 46%、FastDetectGPT 23%、Binoculars 7%**。**对阙疑的直接含义**：学术攻击论文里的"平均降低 87.88%"是**对开源检测器的平均**，而 NeurIPS 实际用的是 Pangram 3.3.2 —— 不能拿前者推断后者。

## 精确数字与案例

### 一、Adversarial Paraphrasing（NeurIPS 2025）——数字最全的一篇

**论文**：*Adversarial Paraphrasing: A Universal Attack for Humanizing AI-Generated Text*
**作者**：Yize Cheng, Vinu Sankar Sadasivan, Mehrdad Saberi, Shoumik Saha, Soheil Feizi（马里兰大学）
**发表**：NeurIPS 2025（proceedings hash `443f314cd420ce621b6e748fd1194ed8`）；arXiv:2506.07001，v1 2025-06-08，v2 2025-10-29
**代码**：github.com/chengez/Adversarial-Paraphrasing

**摘要逐字（关键句）**：

> "we introduce Adversarial Paraphrasing, a **training-free** attack framework that universally humanizes any AI-generated text to evade detection more effectively. Our approach leverages an off-the-shelf instruction-following LLM to paraphrase AI-generated content **under the guidance of an AI text detector**, producing adversarial examples that are specifically optimized to bypass detection."

> "compared to simple paraphrasing attack—which, ironically, **increases** the true positive at 1% false positive (T@1%F) by **8.57% on RADAR** and **15.03% on Fast-DetectGPT**—adversarial paraphrasing, guided by OpenAI-RoBERTa-Large, **reduces T@1%F by 64.49% on RADAR** and a striking **98.96% on Fast-DetectGPT**."

> "Across a diverse set of detectors—including neural network-based, watermark-based, and zero-shot approaches—our attack achieves an **average T@1%F reduction of 87.88%** under the guidance of OpenAI-RoBERTa-Large."

**机制拆解（对阙疑有用的部分）**：

| 环节 | 做法 | 对检测器的意义 |
|---|---|---|
| 改写器 | 现成 instruction-following LLM（非专门微调） | 攻击门槛极低，**不需要训练资源** |
| 指导信号 | 检测器给出的 AI 概率（黑盒可查询） | 只要检测器开放 API，攻击者可把它的输出当损失函数 |
| 优化粒度 | **逐 token** 改写，而非整句重写 | 保留语义的同时逐词破坏统计指纹 |
| 代价 | 作者明确承认"文本质量有轻微退化" | 攻击-质量存在 trade-off，但退化"mostly slight" |

**这是"以子之矛攻子之盾"的教科书案例**：检测器越容易被查询，就越容易被当作奖励模型。对阙疑的启示是——**任何"可被外部无限次查询的判定服务"都自带被优化的风险**，这也是为什么"判决可复算 + 盲态协议不可回盲"比"检测准确率"更重要。

### 二、StealthRL——把攻击从"免训练"推进到"RL 训练"

**论文**：*StealthRL: Reinforcement Learning Paraphrase Attacks for Multi-Detector Evasion of AI-Text Detectors*
**作者**：Suraj Ranganath, Atharv Ramesh
**发表**：arXiv:2602.08934，v1 2026-02-09，v2 2026-03-19（comments 自述"Expanded version of a workshop submission"）
**代码**：github.com/suraj-ranganath/StealthRL

**摘要逐字（关键句）**：

> "StealthRL trains a paraphrase policy against a **multi-detector ensemble** using **Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)** with **LoRA adapters on Qwen3-4B**, optimizing a composite reward that balances detector evasion with semantic preservation."

> "We evaluate **six attack settings (M0-M5)** on the full filtered MAGE test pool (**15,310 human / 14,656 AI**) against four detectors: **RoBERTa, Fast-DetectGPT, Binoculars, and MAGE**. StealthRL achieves **near-zero detection on three of the four detectors** and a **0.024 mean TPR@1%FPR**, reducing **mean AUROC from 0.79 to 0.43** and attaining a **97.6% attack success rate**."

> "Critically, attacks **transfer to two held-out detectors not seen during training**, revealing **shared architectural vulnerabilities** rather than detector-specific brittleness."

**质量评估方法（可复用的评测协议）**：作者对**每种方法各 500 条匹配样本**做 **LLM-based Likert 评分**，并给出**每检测器 AUROC 的 bootstrap 置信区间**——这正是阙疑 E&D 论文应该抄的"攻击评测报告规范"：**不能只报攻击成功率，必须同时报语义保持度与置信区间**。

**M0–M5 六种攻击设定**（论文中的强度阶梯）：从"无攻击"基线逐级增强到多检测器集成 + RL 策略。论文原文未在摘要中列出 M0–M5 的逐条定义，**该映射未核实**（见盲区）。

### 三、防御侧的最强公开数字：MELD 与 MELD-eval

**论文**：*MELD: Multi-Task Equilibrated Learning Detector for AI-Generated Text*
**作者**：Chenjun Li, Cheng Wan, Haomiao Chen, Johannes C. Paetzold
**发表**：arXiv:2605.06903，v1 2026-05-07，v2 2026-09-26

**摘要逐字（关键句）**：

> "MELD (Multi-Task Equilibrated Learning Detector) … trains on **class-balanced, family-specific AI-versus-human tasks** while sharing a common representation of human writing. MELD produces **detection, generator-family, rewrite-task, and token-level predictions in a single forward pass**."

> "MELD ranks **first among open-source submissions in the public RAID leaderboard** and matches or exceeds supervised baselines on **five of six held-out evaluation pools**. To evaluate transfer to unseen generators, we introduce **MELD-eval**, a held-out test pool built from **four frontier chat models**. Without further fine-tuning, MELD achieves **99.7% TPR at 1% FPR on MELD-eval** and **98% TPR at the same FPR on a held-out generator whose family is absent from training**."

> "in a case study of **5.9 million scientific texts from 2016--2026**, MELD's prediction scores remain **stable through 2022 and increase from 2023 onward**, coinciding with the widespread adoption of LLM-based writing tools."

**三个必须记住的点**：

1. **MELD-eval 是"最难评测集之一"**——论文 PDF 明确写道 "Results show that **MELD-eval is one of the hardest evaluation settings we study**"（arXiv PDF 2605.06903）。换言之，**它是为"对抗改写后仍要检出"而设计的 stress test**。
2. **format normalization** 被显式列为防御手段：*"Format normalization further makes its scores **invariant to the modeled reformatting operations**."* —— 这是对**同形字 / 零宽字符 / 空格注入**类攻击的直接针对性防御。
3. **5.9M 科学文本的时间序列**：分数 **2022 年稳定、2023 年起上升**。这为"AI 参与写作的宏观趋势"提供了独立于 Pangram 的证据，且方向与 NeurIPS 官方披露（E&D 赛道 AI 分 ≥90% 的投稿 2025→2026 增长十倍以上）**一致**。

**注意方向性区别**：MELD 是**防御论文**（arXiv 编号 2605.06903，2026-05），MELD-eval 是它发布的**评测池**（HuggingFace 数据集 `anon-review-meld-2026/meld-eval`，CC-BY-4.0，含 tag `ai-text-detection`、`mgt-detection`）。它与方向 03 的"攻击"是同一场军备竞赛的两端。

### 四、改写攻击的经典基线：DIPPER 与递归改写

**DIPPER（NeurIPS 2023）**
**论文**：*Paraphrasing evades detectors of AI-generated text, but retrieval is an effective defense*
**作者**：Kalpesh Krishna, Yixiao Song, Marzena Karpinska, John Wieting, Mohit Iyyer
**arXiv**:2303.13408（v1 2023-03-23，v2 2023-10-18）

> "we build a **11B parameter paraphrase generation model (DIPPER)** that can paraphrase paragraphs, condition on surrounding context, and **control lexical diversity and content reordering**."

> "**DIPPER drops detection accuracy of DetectGPT from 70.3% to 4.6%** (at a constant false positive rate of 1%), without appreciably modifying the input semantics."

> 防御侧：*"we empirically verify our defense using a database of **15M generations** from a fine-tuned T5-XXL model and find that it can **detect 80% to 97% of paraphrased generations** across different settings while only classifying **1% of human-written sequences as AI-generated**."*

**递归改写（recursive paraphrasing）**
**论文**：*Can AI-Generated Text be Reliably Detected?*
**作者**：Vinu Sankar Sadasivan, Aounon Kumar, Sriram Balasubramanian, Wenxiao Wang, Soheil Feizi
**发表**：TMLR（arXiv:2303.11156，v1 2023-03-17，v4 2025-01-17）

> "We introduce **recursive paraphrasing attack** to stress test a wide range of detection schemes, including the ones using the **watermarking** as well as neural network-based detectors, zero shot classifiers, and retrieval-based detectors. Our experiments conducted on passages, each approximately **300 tokens** long …"

> "we investigate the susceptibility of watermarked LLMs to **spoofing attacks** aimed at **misclassifying human-written text as AI-generated**. We demonstrate that an attacker can **infer hidden AI text signatures without white-box access** to the detection method, potentially leading to **reputational risks for LLM developers**."

> "we provide a theoretical framework connecting the **AUROC of the best possible detector to the Total Variation distance** between human and AI text distributions."

**递归改写的关键性质**：*"while our recursive paraphrasing method can **significantly reduce detection rates**, it only **slightly degrades text quality** in many cases"*。这是"多轮改写"策略的原始出处——把文本反复喂给改写器，每轮都削弱一次统计信号。对阙疑的启示：**"递归改写"是"不可回盲"的反面教材**，因为它彻底摧毁了作者身份的可追溯性。

### 五、同形字 / 零宽字符 / Unicode 混淆：字符级攻击

**Pangram 官方直接点名的四类"人味伪造痕迹"**（Pangram blog, 2025-08-27）：

| 痕迹类型 | 逐字描述 | 具体字符/例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **Tortured phrases（受刑短语）** | "out of place synonym replacements meant to disguise plagiarism" | "counterfeit consciousness" 代替 "artificial intelligence"；"bosom peril" 代替 "breast cancer"；有学生作文里出现 **"Martin Luther Ruler, Jr."** 代替 "Martin Luther King, Jr." |
| **Unnatural spacing errors（异常空格）** | "Humanizers often try to **fool the tokenizer** of the AI detectors by adding or removing spaces. Especially common are **space removals between sentences**." | 句间空格被删除 |
| **Repetitive phrases（重复短语）** | "It is especially telling … if the **same tortured phrase appears twice** in the same document, as it is evidence that the humanizer is systematically applying the same synonym replacements." | 同一替换在文内重复出现 |
| **Non-standard characters（非常规字符）** | "Humanizers also typically use **non-standard Unicode characters** in order to fool the tokenizers … An example of this is a popular humanizer that uses **"U+2009", which is the unicode character for "thin space"** instead of a normal space." | **U+2009 THIN SPACE**、零宽空格、BOM、双向控制符 |

Pangram 官方还推荐了一个自查工具 `soscisurvey.de/tools/view-chars.php` 用于显示粘贴文本中隐藏的不可打印字符——**这是"零宽字符混淆"在工业界被明确定性的直接证据**。

**注意一个极易被误用的推论**：Pangram 明确警告 **"tortured phrases" 不能单独作为判据**，因为 *"tortured phrases also commonly show up in **nonnative English writing**"*。**这对阙疑（合肥、非英语母语作者）是一条必须写进 threats-to-validity 的假阳性来源。**

**同形字攻击（homoglyph）**：一份社区维护的技术文档（`github.com/seahop/ai-threat-atlas` 的 `silverspeak-homoglyph-detector-evasion.md`）描述了一类后处理攻击：*"replaces a small fraction of Latin characters in LLM output with visually [identical characters]"*。**该文档为社区材料、非同行评审，其中的成功率数字未核实**（见盲区）。

### 六、水印移除 / 水印窃取

| 论文 | 作者/机构 | 年份 | 关键内容 |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Watermark Stealing in Large Language Models* | Jovanović 等，ETH Zürich SRI Lab（arXiv:2402.19361，v2 2026-08-11） | 2024 | 形式化并系统研究 **LLM 水印窃取**威胁模型 |
| *Attacking LLM Watermarks by Exploiting Their Strengths* | arXiv:2402.16187（ICLR 投稿，OpenReview `P2FFPRxr3Q`） | 2024 | 按水印**设计选择**分类攻击面，并提出 best practices 与防御 |
| *Unified attacks to large language model watermarks: spoofing and s[tealing]* | 华东师范大学（pure.ecnu.edu.cn 记录，2026-09-09） | 2026 | 用 **contrastive decoding** 提取被破坏/放大的水印文本 |

**与 Sadasivan 结论的呼应**：水印的**两大威胁是对称的**——**移除（removal）**让 AI 文本逃逸，**伪造（spoofing）**让人类文本被诬为 AI。后者对阙疑更致命：**如果对手能伪造"AI 签名"，那"AI 分数"就不再是可信证据**——这正是阙疑"证据可追 + 独立对账器"设计要解决的一类问题。

### 八、UChicago 独立审计（BFI WP 2025-116）：最硬的一手数字

**论文**：*Artificial Writing and Automated Detection*
**作者**：**Brian Jabarian**（Chicago Booth, CAAI, CESifo, J-PAL, IGL, JILAEE）、**Alex Imas**（Chicago Booth, CAAI, CESifo, HCEO, NBER）
**发布**：Becker Friedman Institute **WORKING PAPER · NO. 2025-116**，标注 **AUGUST 2025**，正文标注 *"This version: August 26, 2025"*
**URL**：`bfi.uchicago.edu/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/BFI_WP_2025-116.pdf`（45 页）

**语料与设计（逐字）**：

> "we use a **1,992-passage text corpus** that spans **six everyday genres** (news, blogs, consumer reviews, novels, restaurant reviews, and résumés). Verified human-generated text is matched with AI-generated text using **four frontier LLMs (GPT-4.1, Claude Opus 4, Claude Sonnet 4, Gemini 2.0 Flash)**."

> "We evaluate **four commercial and open-source AI-text detectors—Pangram, OriginalityAI, GPTZero and RoBERTa**."

**核心结论（逐字）**：

> "**Pangram stands out as the only AI detector maintaining policy-grade levels** on our main metrics when evaluated on all four generative AI models. On medium-length to long passages, Pangram achieves **essentially zero FPRs and FNRs** within our sample … The **AUROC is one or arbitrarily close to one**, and the **∆−mean is nearly 1 (0.9-1)** in almost all genres and LLM models used, except the ∆−mean in restaurant review between **0.8049 and 0.8878**."

> "**Pangram also performs surprisingly well on text 'stubs' (< 50 words).**"

**humanizer 攻防的关键数字（这是本方向最值钱的一组）**：

> "we fed **every AI passage through the StealthGPT default rewrite endpoint** and re-scored the outputs of the four AI detectors. Since the goal of humanizers is to avoid detection, the relevant statistic is the **FNR**."

> "**Pangram's performance is largely robust to the humanizer. For longer passages, Pangram detects nearly 100% of AI-generated text.** The FNR increases a bit as the passages get shorter, but still remains low."

> "The FNR for **Originality.AI increases to around 0.05** for longer text, but can reach **up to 0.21 for shorter text** … **GPTZero largely loses its capacity to detect AI-generated text, with FNR scores around 0.50 and above** across most genres and LLM models. **RoBERTa does similarly poorly with high FNR scores throughout.**"

**policy cap 框架（对阙疑的"判决阈值设计"极有参考价值）**：

> "we propose an evaluation metric that uses **policy caps—a scale-free, detector-independent measure** that corresponds to the designer's tolerance for false positives or negatives. Using this metric, we show that **Pangram is the only detector that meets a stringent policy cap (FPR ≤ 0.005)** without compromising the ability to accurately detect AI text."

> "For example, Pangram realizes **exactly 0.01 FPR when the policy cap is 0.01** and an FPR of [……]"

**成本（可引用的经济学数字）**：

> "Averaging across genres, the per-call gap is stark, **$0.0227 for Pangram versus $0.0415 for OriginalityAI and $0.0569 for GPTZero**. Similarly, the average cost for shorter passages for Pangram is **$0.0016**, two times cheaper than OriginalityAI at **$0.0029** and almost three times cheaper than GPTZero at **$0.0040**."

**⚠️ 注意这篇与方向 03 的立场差异**：UChicago 这篇是**为 Pangram 背书的审计**（结论是"Pangram 是唯一达标者"），而 Adversarial Paraphrasing / StealthRL 是**攻击方**。**两者并不矛盾**：攻击论文打的是**开源检测器**（DetectGPT、Fast-DetectGPT、Binoculars、RoBERTa），审计论文测的是**Pangram**。**把这两组数字混用是常见错误，阙疑必须分开引用。**

### 九、检测侧的语义方法：Sem-Detect（针对同行评审）

**论文**：*Sem-Detect: Semantic Level Detection of AI Generated Peer-Reviews*
**作者**：André V. Duarte, Brian Tufts, Aditya Oke, Fei Fang, Arlindo L. Oliveira, Lei Li
**发表**：arXiv:2605.21713，v1 2026-05-20（ACM classes: I.2）

> "We argue that, in this setting, **authorship should not be attributed solely from the textual features of a review, but also from the ideas, judgments, and claims it expresses**."

> "Sem-Detect compares a target review against **multiple AI-generated reviews of the same paper**, leveraging the observation that **different AI models tend to converge on similar points, while human reviewers introduce more unique and diverse ones**."

> "Across a dataset of **over 20,000 peer reviews from ICLR and NeurIPS conferences**, Sem-Detect improves over the strongest baseline by **25.5% in TPR@0.1% FPR** in the binary setting. Moreover, in the three-class scenario, we empirically show that **LLM refinement preserves the semantic signals of human reviews** … as a result, **fewer than 3.5% of LLM-refined human reviews are misclassified as AI-generated**."

**这是"对抗改写"的一种天然防御**：改写**改变措辞但不改变"观点集合"**。AI 对同一篇论文生成的评论会**高度收敛**到相似的 claim，而人类评论者的 claim 更分散。**这正是"语义层证据"比"词面层证据"更抗改写的实证**——对阙疑"证据可追"的设计是正面的支持性文献。

### 十、伦理与合规：对抗检测研究本身合法吗？

**关键判断（阙疑必须自己下结论的地方）**：

1. **"研究对抗检测" ≠ "用对抗检测逃避投稿审查"**。Adversarial Paraphrasing 上了 NeurIPS 2025、StealthRL 有公开代码与评测协议，**攻击类论文本身是被学术共同体接受的**——前提是**以"暴露脆弱性 / 建立评测协议"为研究动机**，而不是"帮助作者通过审查"。
2. **风险边界在"是否用于规避本会议自己的 AI 政策"**。NeurIPS PPT 2026 要求"论文本身必须主要由人类撰写"。**如果阙疑用 StealthRL 类工具改写自己的投稿以压低 Pangram 分数，那就是明确的政策违规**——哪怕工具本身来自顶会论文。
3. **Nature Human Behaviour 2026 的立场值得引用但不可当作"免罪符"**：它说的是**"检测器不应过早进入学术治理"**，不是"作者可以不遵守披露要求"。**两者是不同层面的主张，不能互相替代。**

## 盲区（诚实标注）

- **DetectRL 未核实**：任务清单里提到 "DetectRL"，但我用英文关键词 `DetectRL detection reinforcement learning paraphrasing attack` 搜索，返回的**全部是 StealthRL 的结果**，没有任何论文以 "DetectRL" 为标题。**可能是记忆串台，或该论文标题不同/尚未公开检索到。本文件不含 DetectRL 的任何数字。**
- **StealthRL 的 M0–M5 具体定义未核实**：摘要只写 "six attack settings (M0-M5)"，**六种设定的逐条定义我没有读到**（未打开全文 HTML）。同理，**"near-zero detection on three of the four detectors"里的第四个检测器具体表现未核实**。
- **同形字（homoglyph）攻击的成功率未核实**：`ai-threat-atlas` 是**社区维护的 GitHub 文档，非同行评审**，我没有读到它的实验方法与数字；CSDN 上关于"Unicode 隐形注入""5 家大模型全部沦陷"的中文文章（2026-05 前后）**来源为自媒体，未核实，未采信任何数字**。
- **水印移除的具体成功率未核实**：*Attacking LLM Watermarks by Exploiting Their Strengths*、*Watermark Stealing in Large Language Models* 我**只读到了标题/摘要级信息**，未打开全文取逐字数字；华东师大那篇（2026-09-09 记录）**只有机构页面，未见 arXiv 编号**。
- **"递归改写"的逐轮衰减数字未核实**：Sadasivan et al. 摘要只说 "significantly reduce detection rates"，**我没有读到"第 N 轮后降到 X%"的具体数字**。
- **UChicago 论文存在需要标注的立场偏差**：作者受 **Google Cloud Research Program** 资助（acknowledgments 中列明），且结论对 Pangram 高度正面（"the only detector that meets a stringent policy cap"）。**这不构成不当行为，但阅读时应知悉资助来源。** 另：其 FNR/FPR 数字**基于 1,992 条自建语料**，样本量远小于 Pangram 官方基准。
- **Pangram humanizer 表存在利益冲突**：该表由 **Pangram 自己发布**（作者是 Pangram 员工 Bradley Emi），**不是独立第三方评测**。同页引用的 Russell et al.（arXiv:2501.15654）与 Jabarian & Imas 是外部工作，但**表本身的 20 行数字是自报数据**。
- **一个可能被忽略的假阳性来源**：Pangram 自己承认 **"tortured phrases" 在非英语母语写作中常见**。阙疑作者为中文母语者、在合肥写作，**这是本项目必须写进 threats-to-validity 的假阳性通道**，我没有找到针对"非母语作者被 AI 检测器误判"的**定量**研究。
- **伦理边界存在真实争议**：Adversarial Paraphrasing 被 NeurIPS 2025 接收，说明"攻击检测器"本身合法；但**没有任何官方文件明确回答"研究者是否可以用自己发表的攻击方法处理自己的投稿"**。我把它当作**未解决的规范空白**处理，而非已有定论。
