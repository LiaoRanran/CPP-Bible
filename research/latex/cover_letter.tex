To the NeurIPS Datasets \& Benchmarks Chairs and Reviewers,

\bigskip
\noindent
We submit \textbf{``Queyi: Making Verification Capability Independently Auditable''} for
consideration in the \textbf{Datasets \& Benchmarks} track.

\paragraph{Why this fits E\&D.}
Our contribution is an \emph{evaluation methodology} with a fully reproducible artifact and an
explicit \emph{critical / negative} analysis---the three things E\&D asks for. We do not claim a new
model or a new benchmark. We claim a way to make the question ``how well can we verify
LLM-generated technical knowledge?'' an \emph{externally checkable} one.

\paragraph{Core contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{A verification system} built on four-state verdicts
        (\texttt{pass}/\texttt{fail}/\texttt{unknown}/\texttt{contradict}, with \texttt{unknown} a
        first-class citizen), a forced split of provenance vs.\ semantic scope, and a
        \emph{kernel-independent} meta-state reconciler (anti-self-justification).
  \item \textbf{An evaluation protocol}: a five-layer dataset (D0--D4), budget-matched baselines, a
        six-group ablation with pre-registered hypotheses, and a \emph{frozen} statistical caliber
        (Clopper--Pearson primary; Fisher/Boschloo; exact McNemar; BH/Holm).
  \item \textbf{An open artifact}: a Merkle-anchored supply chain, a 452-event authority ledger,
        per-sample detail artifacts, and single-command recomputation entry points.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Relation to prior work.}
Benchmark-evolution lines (Benchmark Self-Evolving, ArenaBencher, LiveBench) evolve the
\emph{items}; mutation-guided testing (Meta ACH, MUTGEN) evolves \emph{tests}; formal verification
(seL4, CompCert) provides \emph{proofs}. \textbf{None evolves the measuring stick itself while
proving the change is externally measurable.} That is our position.

\paragraph{Honest limitations (stated up front).}
Two headline numbers depend on a specific WSL sanitizer environment and \textbf{fail silently} when
it is absent (35.0\% $\to$ 10.0\%); sample sizes are small ($n=16/40$); and \textbf{budget-matched
baseline experiments are in progress and will be included in the camera-ready version}. We
therefore claim \textbf{auditability of the mechanism}, \textbf{not} superiority over existing
methods. We include this analysis as part of the contribution rather than hiding it.

\paragraph{Data and code availability.}
The repository is public (Apache-2.0) with DCO sign-off. Datasets D2/D3/D4 and the recomputation
commands are described in \texttt{REPLICATION.md}; Croissant metadata will be provided in the
supplementary material. Every reported number is recomputed from landed artifacts, and the paper
marks any not-yet-landed value explicitly rather than filling in estimates.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
All headline numbers are reproducible via single commands; the paper documents the exact
environment dependencies (WSL, \texttt{g++}, \texttt{setarch}) and reports \texttt{UNVERIFIED}
rather than a misleading low score when a dependency is missing.
