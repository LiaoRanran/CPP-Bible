\noindent
We submit \textbf{``Auditing the Evaluator: Stress-Testing Evidence-Based Software
Verification''} for consideration in the \textbf{Evaluations \& Datasets (E\&D)} track,
renamed from Datasets \& Benchmarks in 2026 and (since 2026) \textbf{typically double-blind};
we target the 2027 edition. \emph{Throughout, ``verifier'' is used in the
evidence-acquisition sense}: a \texttt{catch} verdict reports that refuting evidence was
acquired within our capability boundary, \textbf{not} that the assertion is semantically true.

The 2027 CFP is not yet released. This draft uses the official \texttt{neurips\_2025} style as a

\paragraph{Why this fits E\&D.}
Our contribution is an \emph{evaluation methodology} with a fully reproducible artifact and a
substantial \emph{negative} result---the audit shows several apparently convincing evaluator
claims weakening or collapsing. We claim no new detector and no algorithmic superiority. Our
question: \emph{how often can an apparently convincing evaluation result survive an audit of
the evaluator that produced it?}

\paragraph{Three contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{An auditable protocol for evaluating evaluators.} Every quantitative claim
        declares a measurement caliber $Q=(D,A,E,\Theta,P)$ (dataset, asset set, environment
        set, configuration set, protocol); eight failure modes provide identification signals;
        each headline claim is re-measured under adversarial controls that change no malicious
        behavior, and receives one of four audit outcomes (\textbf{survives / weakened /
        collapses / unresolved}).
  \item \textbf{A systematic empirical audit} of a software-verification apparatus:
        \textbf{1147} self-authored samples $\times$ \textbf{8} assets plus \textbf{110}
        source-derived CVE reconstructions (all sample CVEs NVD-verified online), with
        clone-aware splitting and explicit environment profiles.
  \item \textbf{Quantitative findings on what survives.} (F1)~A $+24.0$pp selection gain is
        \emph{mostly measurement-pool composition}; removing degenerate assets leaves
        $+7.4$--$11.3$pp (mechanism level). (F2)~The instrument is \emph{blind to 38.4\%} of
        its corpus in a family-structured way; severity does not predict detection; 41.5\% of
        real-defect catches ride on a single asset (\texttt{asan}). (F3)~\emph{Environment is
        part of the measurement}: without the declared WSL profile, real-defect detection
        falls $59.09\%\to23.64\%$ with \emph{no} unknown increase and no warning. (F4)~Synthetic
        and source-derived corpora \emph{do not reach TOST equivalence} at $\pm10$pp (90\% CI
        $[-10.61,+5.52]$pp), and standardization shows the similar headline rates hide a
        $-17.9$pp composition offset. (F5)~Failure-driven evolution yields \emph{no measurable
        recall gain} over frequency-based selection (identical selections in 14/14 tier checks;
        best $+1.41$pp, $p{=}0.302$)---reported as an audit finding, not a contribution.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{What we deliberately do \emph{not} claim.}
No priority (``first to audit an evaluator''---DeepFact~\cite{huang2026deepfact}, metric
co-evolution~\cite{zhang2026whogrades}, certified self-evolution~\cite{sengupta2026anytimevalid}
and SV-COMP's witness validation~\cite{beyer2026svcomp} are active neighbours); no superiority
over mature verifiers (our cross-tool comparison is \emph{observational}, and the pre-registered
caliber failed at chance level); no semantic verification (a \texttt{catch} is acquired evidence
within a boundary); and \textbf{no human label validation}---human IAA is 0; the $\kappa$ values
in the paper are AI self-consistency; a de-identified 145-sample annotation package with
pre-registered thresholds accompanies the submission and its execution is the top post-review
priority.

\paragraph{Assets, statistics and reproducibility.}
Apache-2.0, DCO-signed; fail-loud reproduction scripts; all headline numbers recomputed from
frozen matrices by two new deterministic scripts (equivalence/standardization; environment
metrics); the annotation package and per-sample artifacts ship as supplementary material.
Known limits, declared in the paper: the primary profile is WSL-bound (the native profile's
sanitizer absence is an architectural inference; clang-cl/MSVC were not tested); template-clone
dependence (design effect $\approx4.2$); $\sim$5\% per-cell run-to-run instability; the
eight-asset instrument scope.

beyond C++ verification.
