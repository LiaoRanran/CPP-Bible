To the NeurIPS Evaluations \& Datasets Chairs and Reviewers,

\bigskip
\noindent
We submit \textbf{``Caliber Drift and Capability Boundaries: An Audit Protocol for
Software-Verification Evaluation''} to the \textbf{Evaluations \& Datasets (E\&D)} track
(2027 edition). \emph{Throughout, ``verifier'' is used in the
evidence-acquisition sense}: a \texttt{catch} reports refuting evidence acquired within
our capability boundary, \textbf{not} semantic truth.

\paragraph{Template note.}
The 2027 CFP is unreleased; this draft uses the official \texttt{neurips\_2025} style as
an unmodified placeholder with a neutral ``Under review'' notice.

\paragraph{Why this fits E\&D.}
An \emph{evaluation methodology} with a reproducible artifact and a substantial
\emph{negative} result. We claim no new detector and no algorithmic superiority. Our
question: \emph{how often can an apparently convincing evaluation result survive an audit
of the evaluator that produced it?}

\paragraph{Four contributions.}
(1)~\textbf{A measurement-drift algebra}: seven axioms and six theorems; our metatheory
shows only \textbf{two} axioms are independent (monotonicity, super-additivity), four are
theorems and one is a meta-axiom, and the system is \textbf{incomplete}---label drift
(Type~III) cannot be expressed, so we register a missing \textbf{A8 (label-axis closure)}
and prove caliber arbitrage NP-hard with a greedy $(1{-}1/e)$ approximation.
(2)~\textbf{Structural (passive) Goodhart}, in four types
(caliber/composition/label/aggregation), each moving the reported number with \emph{no}
optimisation pressure; Types~III and IV leave \emph{every} per-sample verdict unchanged.
(3)~\textbf{A capability-boundary map}: the instrument is blind to \textbf{38.4\%}
($440/1147$) of its corpus, \textbf{13 of 34} defect types exceed $50\%$ blindness, and
\textbf{41.5\%} of real-defect catches ride on one asset.
(4)~\textbf{Real-CVE validation}: \textbf{110} NVD-verified CVE reconstructions detect at
\textbf{59.09\%}.

\paragraph{Key audited numbers.}
A $+24.03$pp apparent selection gain \emph{collapses at $k{=}4$} ($0.00$pp, $p{=}1.0$)
once degenerate assets are removed, leaving $+7.4$--$11.3$pp. Under the
environment-aware protocol the declared profile gives $60.07\%$ and the reduced profile
$24.74\%$ (566-sample split), with $\Delta$unknown $=0.00$pp unaware versus $75.27$pp
aware---a \emph{silent} degradation. The evolution operator is $\Delta{=}0$ at
\emph{every} tier (identical to greedy set-cover). Paired designs have identically
\textbf{zero power} against Types~III/IV ($\psi{=}0$), so Step~3 becomes a \emph{dual}
design: sample-level pairing for I/II (Type~I needs $n\approx849$, Type~II only $17$),
design-level contrast for III/IV.

\paragraph{What we do \emph{not} claim.}
No priority (DeepFact, metric co-evolution, certified self-evolution and SV-COMP witness
validation are neighbours); no superiority over mature verifiers; no semantic
verification; and \textbf{no human label validation}---human IAA is \textbf{0}, all
$\kappa$ values are AI self-consistency, and a de-identified 145-sample annotation package
ships unexecuted. Two gaps stay open: the axiom system is \textbf{incomplete} (A8
missing), and of \textbf{22} original open-source projects collected for cross-domain
validation \textbf{none has been compiled} (no network egress); build feasibility is
untested.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
Apache-2.0, DCO-signed, fail-loud scripts; every headline number recomputed from frozen
matrices. Declared limits: a WSL-bound primary profile (the native profile's sanitizer
absence is an architectural inference; clang-cl/MSVC untested); template-clone dependence
(design effect $\approx4.2$); $\sim$5\% per-cell run instability; an eight-asset scope.

We hope the E\&D community finds the \textbf{audit protocol and negative findings}
useful beyond C++ verification.
