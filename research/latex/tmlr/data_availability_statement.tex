\section{Data and Code Availability}

This work's empirical claims are backed by fully released artifacts. To preserve
double-blind review, an \textbf{anonymous snapshot} of the repository (stripped of
author identity, institution, and internal path strings) is provided as supplementary
material; the public version is released upon acceptance.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Holdout \& corpus benchmarks.} 41 holdout and 64 corpus measurable
        samples with per-sample provenance, at
        \texttt{data/holdout\_reveal\_5\_672h.json} and
        \texttt{data/external\_corpus\_reveal\_672h.json}.
  \item \textbf{A5 falsification experiment.} The full $1137\times 8$ detection matrix
        and the clone-aware / non-degenerate-pool re-runs at
        \texttt{data/a5\_676f\_matrix\_*.jsonl}, \texttt{data/677b\_a5\_results\_*.json},
        \texttt{data/677c\_a5\_nondegenerate\_results.json}.
  \item \textbf{Blind-spot map.} $1147\times 8$ capability-boundary matrix at
        \texttt{data/blindspot\_676g\_stats.json}.
  \item \textbf{Reproduction entry point.} \texttt{REPRODUCE.md} and
        \texttt{bash docker/paper/run\_all.sh} (fail-loud; any inconsistency exits non-zero).
        Every headline number has a one-line recompute command
        (\texttt{tools/verify\_paper\_numbers.py}; 118 checks / 113 consistent / 0 hard errors).
  \item \textbf{Trust anchor.} An append-only ledger of 452 events with a Merkle root
        (\texttt{tools/tool\_integrity.py}) covers the controlled directories; no
        post-hoc editing of verified evidence is possible.
\end{itemize}

All datasets are released under a permissive license suitable for benchmarking; no
personal data is contained. Compute is a standard x64 machine with g++/clang and WSL;
the sanitizer branch depends on WSL, and its absence silently downgrades 15 samples to
\texttt{unknown} (registered as a known environment bias, not a hideable defect).
