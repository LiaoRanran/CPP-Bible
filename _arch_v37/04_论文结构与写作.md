## 3. 三个关键写作动作

### 3.1 动机节：三案例开局【已查证：全部有源】

半页三个真实事件：(a) MCP 工具投毒跨会话泄密（Invariant 2025-04）[v35-S3]；(b) Arena 刷榜产业化（Llama-4 事件+200 万对战分析）[v35-S11]；(c) TruthfulQA 47% 题面可在线检索（BenchMarker）[v35-S16]。收束句："攻击者已经转向验证层；而验证层的失败是静默的——没有人审计审计者。" 禁止"LLM 不可靠"式空泛开局。

### 3.2 novelty 三段式（模板直接填）

"Prior work audited judges for accuracy (A: MT-Bench/JudgeBench/CodeJudgeBench/BabelJudge), catalogued attacks on AI systems (B: ATLAS/MCPTox/ImpossibleBench), and hardened infrastructure integrity (C: Sigstore/in-toto/de Bruijn criterion). None audits whether a verification *process* has been subverted on *production evidence* with *fail-closed semantics* — because doing so requires a real, append-only decision ledger plus an architecture in which verifiers cannot self-certify (Y). Queyi (Z) provides exactly these: 452 real adjudication events under Merkle anchoring, a 9-protector fail-closed core, and an independent re-implementation channel — validated by a real catch (G12) that differential verification exposed."

### 3.3 摘要黄金 30 秒（150 词五句模板）

1. 问题句：Verification layers for LLM outputs are becoming the attack target（+一个案例数字）。
2. 缺口句：Existing audits are benchmark-facing; no one audits the verification process itself under attack.
3. 系统句：We present Queyi, a fail-closed meta-verification architecture (five layers, nine protectors, 813-line kernel) for executable knowledge claims.
4. 数字句：On a production ledger of 452 adjudication events, meta-monitoring catches all 50 injected mutations; escape rate 0.071% (95% CI [x, y]); poisoned/abstain suites: zero silent passes.
5. artifact 句：All code, ledger, and benchmark are openly available with one-command replay.

**风格纪律**（顶会系统文 vs 科普）：动词精确（catch/expose/anchor，不 fight/solve）；每个形容词后必须能跟一个数字；不出现"首次/完美/绝对"除非有形式化背书；被动少、句短；诚实限制写在 Discussion 而不是藏起来——审稿人对自觉披露的宽容度远高于被抓包。

## 4. 阙疑应该怎么做（3 条）

1. **先写 §5 Evaluation 的空壳（表头+占位行）再写其他节**：实验表提前定型会反向约束实验设计，避免"写完才发现缺一列"的返工；这也是 artifact 脚本的接口定义。
2. **标题定稿方向**："Verifying the Verifiers: Fail-Closed Meta-Verification for Executable Knowledge Claims"（v34-06 §2.2 已立，本篇确认加副标题 "with a Production Adjudication Ledger" 把最稀缺资产写进标题）。
3. **每写完一节跑一次"数字核对清单"**：47/452/0.0711%/50/50/95.2%/0.9851 各出现处与冻结契约口径一致（v37-03 §3 口径纪律）——数字不一致是 desk reject 级错误。
